Our story

It all started with Boycie & Beau

Our club is named after the two characters who rule our own home: Boycie, a big-hearted blue Staffy who has never met a walk he didn't love, and Beau, a dignified British Shorthair who very much runs the household.

Caring for them taught us what pet owners really want — someone reliable, someone who genuinely cares, and someone who'll treat their home and their animals with total respect. That's exactly what we offer every member of the club.

Good questions

Frequently asked

Do you offer a free meet & greet?

Always. Before any booking we'll come and meet you and your pet at home, completely free and with no obligation. It's the best way for everyone to feel comfortable.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover our local area and the surrounding neighbourhoods. Send us your postcode and we'll confirm straight away.

Are you insured?

Yes — we're fully insured for pet care, including care, custody and control of your pets and key cover for your home.

How will I know how the visit went?

After every walk or visit you'll receive a message with photos and a quick update on how your pet got on — eating, toilet, mood and any mischief.

Can you give my pet medication?

Yes, we're happy to give tablets, liquid medication and most routine treatments. Just let us know at your meet & greet.

How do I book and pay?

Book by message, phone or the form below. We'll send a simple invoice — bank transfer is easiest, and regular clients can pay weekly or monthly.
